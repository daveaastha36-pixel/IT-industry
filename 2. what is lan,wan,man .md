##LAN : LOCAL AREA NETWORK##
***Dfinition: a network that connects computers and devices within a small geographic area.***
***ex. computers connected in a school, home , computer lab , or office.***

##WAN: WIDE AREA NETWORK ##
***Definition***: a network that covers a large geographical area such as different cities , countries or continents .
***ex. the internet*** 

##MAN : METROPOLITAN AREA NETWORK ##
***Definition : a network that covers a city or large campus connecting multiple LANs.***
***ex. a network connecting different branches of a bank across ahmedabad.***
